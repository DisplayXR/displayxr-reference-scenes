# DisplayXR 3D-display test scenes

Scenes that test the **display path** (stereo/multiview geometry, the
zero-disparity plane (ZDP), the display edges, crosstalk) rather than
materials. Generated by
[`scripts/make_display_tests.py`](../../scripts/make_display_tests.py),
deterministic (CI regenerates them and requires identical bytes), and
dedicated to the public domain (CC0-1.0).

| File | What to look for |
|---|---|
| `depth_ladder.glb` | Nine checker tiles, each labelled with its depth in display heights (vH), from +0.40 (in front of the glass) to −0.40 (behind). The `0.00vH` tile must show no parallax. Each step should read as an even depth increment. |
| `window_box.glb` | An open-fronted grid box. Its opening is the frame at the ZDP and its back wall sits 0.40 vH behind. Move your head: the box must stay put and its grid lines must stay straight. A swimming or bending box is a view-rig / Kooima fault. |
| `edge_violation.glb` | Bars crossing the top and bottom frame edges at depths from +0.40 to −0.40 vH. Run with `--vh 1.0` so the frame edges coincide with the display edges. A **red** bar (in front of the glass) cut by the edge is a stereo window violation. A **green** one (behind) is not. |
| `crosstalk_bars.glb` | Thin self-lit white bars over a black backdrop at the same depths. Crosstalk shows as a faint double of each bar, growing with distance from the ZDP. The `0.00vH` bar must show none. |

**Framing.** Every scene is 1.4 wide × 1.0 tall, with its bounding box centred
on the origin. Under the DisplayXR fit rule (box centre on the ZDP, height
filled to 80%), 1 display height is exactly 1.25 scene units on any panel at
least ~1.74:1 wide, so the labels are exact. On a narrower (e.g. portrait)
panel, pass `--vh 1.25` to restore that mapping.

Regenerate: `python3 scripts/make_display_tests.py scenes/displayxr-display-tests/assets`.
